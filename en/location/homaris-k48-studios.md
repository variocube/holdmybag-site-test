## Opening hours

Open 24/7.

## Booking and payment

- Online: Reserve and pay for a locker in advance; the access code arrives by email. [Book now](https://booking.holdmybag.net/homaris-ag/homaris-apartmenthaus-coe48/dates)
- On site by smartphone: Scan the QR code on the locker, choose a compartment and pay on your phone.
- On site at the terminal: not available at this location.
