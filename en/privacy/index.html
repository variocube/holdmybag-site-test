Privacy Policy

for the use of the lockers (“Locker”) of Holdmybag GmbH via website, QR code and display on site

As of: 30 September 2025

The protection of your personal data is important to us. Below we inform you about the processing of your data in connection with the use of our digital locker solutions via the website, QR codes and displays on site, in accordance with the provisions of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the German Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG).

2. Subject matter of the data processing

Holdmybag GmbH provides users with digitally controlled lockers at various locations (hotels, event venues, public places, its own locker stores, etc.). Access is available, at your choice, via:

  • our website (e.g. www.holdmybag.net),
  • QR codes at the locker stations or in digital media,
  • and touch displays directly at the locker installation.

3. Categories of personal data

When you use our lockers, the following personal data may be processed, depending on the booking and access route you choose:

Payment dataTransaction number, payment service provider, means of payment (pseudonymised only) – not accessible to service providers such as location partners
Usage dataIP address, browser data, device type

5. Recipients of the data

As a matter of principle, your data is not passed on to third parties, unless this is necessary to perform the contract or is required by law. Recipients may in particular be:

  • IT and cloud service providers (e.g. Variocube GmbH – for technical locker management)
  • Payment service providers (e.g. Stripe, Mollie – we do not process complete account data)
  • Location partners (e.g. hotels or festival organisers – access to customer data only in support cases and exclusively on the basis of a data processing agreement)
  • Support service providers (first- and second-level)
  • Authorities and courts (where legally required)

6. Commissioned data processing

Where third parties (e.g. location partners or technical service providers) gain access to personal data, this takes place exclusively on the basis of a data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR. Such access is provided exclusively to support the end customer in using the lockers, and exclusively as read-only access.

7. Data processing when using QR codes and displays

The following special features apply when you use a QR code or the display on site:

  • The display is shown dynamically via a secured connection to the backend system.
  • An anonymous or pseudonymous session is recorded.
  • Data entered manually (e.g. name, phone number) is recorded directly in the system.
  • A token or temporary session ID may be generated for use.

9. Your rights

You have the right at any time to:

  • access to your stored data (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • object to the processing (Art. 21 GDPR)

You can demand that we erase your personal data if we process it unlawfully or if its processing/publication disproportionately interferes with your legitimate interests worthy of protection. Please note that there may be reasons that prevent immediate erasure, e.g. in the case of statutory retention obligations.

10. Withdrawal of consent

If you have given us consent to data processing (e.g. for feedback surveys), you can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future.

11. Data protection supervision

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the Member State of your habitual residence or of the alleged infringement.

Competent supervisory authority:

The Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information
https://datenschutz.hessen.de/

12. Data security

We use technical and organisational measures to protect your data against manipulation, loss, destruction or unauthorised access. These include, among others, encrypted data transmission, access restrictions and regular audits.

13. Changes to this privacy policy

This privacy policy is reviewed regularly and amended where necessary. The current version is available on our website or can be viewed on site via the display.

14. Severability clause

Should one or more provisions of this policy be invalid, unlawful or otherwise ineffective, this does not render the entire contract or the entire terms invalid. The invalid provision will be replaced by the relevant statutory provision. Holdmybag GmbH reserves the right to amend the general provisions at any time.
