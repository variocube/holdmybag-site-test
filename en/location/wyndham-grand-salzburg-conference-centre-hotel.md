## Getting there and access

Access is via the hotel's main entrance and the parking garage. Luggage lockers are located in the stairwell. Operating hours correspond to the hotel's opening hours.

## Opening hours

Open 24/7.

## Booking and payment

- Online: Reserve and pay for a locker in advance; the access code arrives by email. [Book now](https://booking.holdmybag.net/wyndham-salzburg/wyndham-hotel-salzburg/dates)
- On site by smartphone: Scan the QR code on the locker, choose a compartment and pay on your phone. Payment methods: Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal.
- On site at the terminal: Choose a compartment on the screen and pay by card or contactless at the terminal. Payment methods: Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay.
