## Getting there and access

The lockers are open and accessible daily from 7:00 AM to 11:59 PM. On-site emergency support is available from 7:00 AM until approximately 10:00 PM. After this time, emergency locker opening cannot be guaranteed. In case of an emergency outside support hours, assistance will only be available the following day at the information desk of the Vienna Giant Ferris Wheel, Riesenradplatz 2 / Gabor-Steiner-Weg, 1020 Vienna.

## Booking and payment

- Online: Reserve and pay for a locker in advance; the access code arrives by email. [Book now](https://booking.safecube.io/wienerriesenrad/wienerriesenrad/dates)
- On site by smartphone: Scan the QR code on the locker, choose a compartment and pay on your phone. Payment methods: Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal.
- On site at the terminal: Choose a compartment on the screen and pay by card or contactless at the terminal. Payment methods: Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal.

## About this location

The new Holdmybag / Variocube luggage lockers are located directly next to the entrance of the Vienna Giant Ferris Wheel (Wiener Riesenrad) in the Vienna Prater.

Lockers are available in various sizes, including options for large suitcases. The minimum rental period is 2 hours and can be extended at any time online or directly at the touchscreen display.

The lockers are accessible daily from 7:00 AM to 11:59 PM. On-site support is available for emergencies from 7:00 AM until approximately 10:00 PM. After this time, emergency locker opening cannot be guaranteed.

In case of an emergency outside support hours, assistance will only be available the following day at the information desk of the Vienna Giant Ferris Wheel, Riesenradplatz 2 / Gabor-Steiner-Weg, 1020 Vienna.
