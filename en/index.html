HoldMyBag stands for modern, secure and smart luggage lockers at central locations

  • Every location has real, lockable compartments – no unsecured storage in cafés or shops.
  • Central locations in shopping centres, hotels and event venues.
  • Book directly on-site or, at many locations, even online in advance – so you skip the trip to fully occupied lockers.
  • You'll find the fees in the overview for each location – some lockers are even free.
  • Collecting your luggage later? You can simply book additional time.
  • Different compartment sizes for large and small luggage.
