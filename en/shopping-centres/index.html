The service that extends the shopping experience

A small service with a big impact

Shopping centres have long been more than places to shop – they are meeting points, leisure spots and experience worlds. Success today depends heavily on dwell time and visitor satisfaction. Whoever feels comfortable stays longer – and spends more.

Benefits for visitors

  • Digital, easy-to-use lockers
  • Bookable instantly at the terminal or online
  • Contactless payment (card, smartphone or QR code)
  • Content insurance included
  • Different compartment sizes – from handbag to suitcase
  • 24/7 access possible (depending on location)

Benefits for centre management

  • Central management & real-time overview of all installations
  • Automatic operation – no staff required
  • Flexible usage concepts: free, paid or hybrid
  • Modular design – adaptable to the centre's space and look
  • Branding options: logo, colours or ads on the display
  • Monitoring & remote maintenance via the cloud
More comfort = longer visits = higher revenue

The right location makes the difference

In many centres, lockers are still placed in remote spots – often near the toilets. That leads to low usage. A modern service only works where people actually are: at the entrance, along main walkways or between food court and shops. Centrally placed lockers are used far more often – and are a real plus for families with children, backpacks and winter clothing.

If a visitor forgets to empty their compartment, the system can automatically notify them via their stored contact details – collection the next day stays convenient, with no effort for centre staff.
