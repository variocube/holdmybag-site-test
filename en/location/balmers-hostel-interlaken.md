## Booking and payment

- Online: Reserve and pay for a locker in advance; the access code arrives by email. [Book now](https://booking.holdmybag.net/balmers-hostel/balmers-hostel-interlaken/dates)
- On site by smartphone: Scan the QR code on the locker, choose a compartment and pay on your phone. Payment methods: Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, TWINT.
- On site at the terminal: Choose a compartment on the screen and pay by card or contactless at the terminal. Payment methods: Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, TWINT.
