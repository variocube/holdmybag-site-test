## Getting there and access

Zugang über den Eingang zum Apartment Haus (Haupteingang) von der Mondescheingasse aus. Dann auf der Treppe in das Untergeschoss.

## Opening hours

Open 24/7.

## Booking and payment

- Online: Reserve and pay for a locker in advance; the access code arrives by email. [Book now](https://booking.holdmybag.net/three-friends/heymi-apartments/dates)
- On site by smartphone: Scan the QR code on the locker, choose a compartment and pay on your phone. Payment methods: Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal.
- On site at the terminal: Choose a compartment on the screen and pay by card or contactless at the terminal. Payment methods: Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay.
